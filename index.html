Main finding: the Savannah pages are being seen, but almost nobody clicks. They earned 3,199 impressions and 19 clicks, a 0.59% click-through rate. 88% of impressions came from pages averaging position 11 or lower, which is page two of Google or beyond. The home, contact and about pages produced 14 of the 19 clicks and 6 of the 9 phone clicks. The commercial, service and city pages, which target the money keywords, produced 5 clicks between them.

What is working

  • The Savannah home page ranks at an average of 6.6, earns the most search clicks (8) and drives 5 of the 9 phone clicks.
  • Storm damage searches reach page one: "storm damage repair" (4.8), "roof repair after storm damage savannah ga" (5.5), "storm damage restoration savannah ga" (6.7).
  • Windows page visitors are the most engaged in the section: 92% engagement and almost 3 minutes on page, with 1 phone click.

What needs attention

  • The Commercial page earned 1,051 impressions, 33% of the total, with zero clicks at an average position of 19.6.
  • City pages earned 828 impressions with zero clicks. Pooler (23.8) and Richmond Hill (22.5) rank on page three, and city pages received only 14 page views.
  • Some searches are matched to the wrong page. "gutter installation richmond hill ga" ranks with the Statesboro gutter page, and "siding installation pooler ga" ranks with the Statesboro siding page.

What this shows

  • AI visitors are the highest-quality traffic in the section: 3 phone clicks from 10 sessions, compared with 3 from 115 organic search sessions.
  • ChatGPT is the only AI tool sending meaningful traffic. Gemini and Perplexity sent one visit each to the whole site and none to Savannah.
  • ChatGPT also sent visitors to two Savannah URLs that do not exist (/savannah/windows and /savannah/wiundows), so it is guessing at page addresses for some answers.
  • 8 of the 10 sessions came from desktop users in the Wilmington Island area, so the sample is small and may include people testing the site.
  • Two searches read like AI Mode questions, "can you list steps involved in professional window installation?" and "how long has he been in business?". Both show a Savannah page at position 1, which fits the page being cited as a source in an AI answer. The Meet the Team page is being used to answer questions about the business itself.
  • Google AI Overviews are not reported separately. Any impressions or clicks from them are already inside the Search Console figures above.

Recommendations

Each recommendation is tied to what the data shows.

  1. Rebuild the Commercial page to win page oneHigh
    Data: 1,051 impressions (33% of all), 0 clicks, average position 19.6. It ranks for "commercial roofing savannah ga" (76 impr., pos. 24.8), "commercial roof repair savannah ga" (61, 17.0), "commercial roof leak repair savannah ga" (56, 15.3) and "savannah ga commercial roof replacement" (57, 14.5).
    Do: Give repair, leak repair, replacement, reroofing, inspection and maintenance their own sections with Savannah project photos, building types served (warehouses, offices, port facilities) and commercial reviews. Put "Commercial Roof Repair & Replacement in Savannah, GA" in the title. Request reindexing; Google last crawled it Sep 21.
  2. Make the city pages genuinely localHigh
    Data: 828 impressions across 10 city URLs and 0 clicks. Pooler averages position 23.8, Richmond Hill 22.5, Statesboro 19.6. These are the pages that lost the most visibility after Google's spam update finished on Oct 8.
    Do: Add content no other CR3 location could use: neighborhoods and subdivisions served, finished jobs in that town with photos, reviews from customers there, and local weather and code notes. Link each city page from the matching service pages, not only the menu.
  3. Fix pages ranking for the wrong searchesHigh
    Data: "gutter installation richmond hill ga" (10 impr., pos. 10.1) shows the Statesboro gutter page. "siding installation pooler ga" (8, pos. 6.8) shows the Statesboro siding page. "roof replacement savannah ga" (14, pos. 7.6) shows the Commercial page. A searcher in Richmond Hill or Pooler sees a page about a different city.
    Do: Add gutter, siding and roofing sections to the Richmond Hill and Pooler pages, or build city service pages like Statesboro has. Link "roof replacement" wording on the home and Commercial pages to Residential Roofing.
  4. Turn page-one rankings into clicksHigh
    Data: 8 non-branded searches rank in positions 5 to 10 with 146 impressions and 0 clicks. Most go to the Storm Damage page, which has 305 impressions and 1 click.
    Do: Rewrite titles and meta descriptions on Storm Damage, Residential Roofing, Gutter Systems and Siding. Lead with the service and "Savannah, GA", and add 24/7 response, insurance help and the phone number.
  5. Build out window repair contentMedium
    Data: "window repair savannah" (64 impr.) and "window repair savannah ga" (30) rank around position 27 with the Windows page, which averages 19.4 across 188 impressions.
    Do: Add a window repair section (broken seals, foggy glass, frames, sills, wood rot) with FAQs to the Windows page.
